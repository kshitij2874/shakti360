Scholarships for Young Women in India (Ages 11-24)

India offers numerous scholarships specifically for girls and young women to support education from secondary school through higher education and professional courses.

Central Government Scholarships:

1. CBSE Single Girl Child Merit Scholarship:
- For single girl children who are the only child of their parents.
- Class X CGPA 6.2 or above in CBSE Board.
- Amount: Rs.500/month for 2 years (Class XI-XII).
- Apply: cbse.gov.in.

2. Pragati Scholarship (AICTE):
- For girls pursuing technical education (engineering, pharmacy, architecture, planning).
- One girl per family; family income below Rs.8 lakh/year.
- Amount: Rs.50,000/year (tuition) + Rs.2,000/month for 10 months.
- Up to 5,000 scholarships per year.
- Apply: scholarships.gov.in.

3. AICTE Saksham Scholarship:
- For differently-abled students including girls in technical courses.
- Amount: Rs.50,000/year + Rs.2,000/month.

4. Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC Girls:
- Central sector scheme covering tuition, maintenance, and books.
- Family income ceiling: Rs.2.5 lakh (SC/ST), Rs.1.5 lakh (OBC).
- Apply through state government portals.

5. Begum Hazrat Mahal National Scholarship:
- For meritorious girls from minority communities.
- Class IX-XII students with minimum 50% marks.
- Amount: Rs.5,000 (IX-X), Rs.6,000 (XI-XII).

6. INSPIRE Scholarship (DST):
- For top 1% performers in Class XII board exams pursuing science.
- Amount: Rs.80,000/year for 5 years (BSc/Integrated MSc).
- 50% seats reserved for girls.

7. Kishore Vaigyanik Protsahan Yojana (KVPY):
- Now merged with INSPIRE, for students pursuing basic sciences.
- Monthly fellowship of Rs.5,000-7,000.

State Government Scholarships (Selected):
- Karnataka Vidyasiri: Rs.1,500-5,000/year for SC/ST/OBC girls.
- UP Kanya Vidya Dhan: Rs.30,000 one-time for Class XII pass girls.
- West Bengal Kanyashree Prakalpa: Rs.25,000 one-time at age 18 for unmarried girls.
- Rajasthan Garandiha Bai Scholarship: for Class 10-12 girls in government schools.
- Maharashtra Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj Scholarship: for economically weaker girls.

Private and Corporate Scholarships:
- Tata Trusts Scholarship: for Class 10+ girls from low-income families.
- Kotak Kanya Scholarship: up to Rs.1.5 lakh/year for professional courses (family income below Rs.6 lakh).
- Fair and Lovely (Glow and Lovely) Scholarship: Rs.2.5 lakh for post-Class 12 girls.
- Google Women Techmakers Scholarship: USD 1,000 for women in CS/tech.
- Adobe India Women-in-Tech Scholarship: Rs.1 lakh for BTech CS/IT students.

International Scholarships for Indian Women:
- Commonwealth Scholarships: fully funded Masters/PhD in UK universities.
- Chevening Scholarships: UK government funded, for future leaders.
- DAAD (Germany): various programmes for Indian women in STEM.
- Fulbright-Nehru: for Masters/PhD in the USA.

How to Apply — Step by Step:
1. Visit scholarships.gov.in (National Scholarship Portal) or specific scheme websites.
2. Register with Aadhaar and mobile number.
3. Fill application with academic details and family income proof.
4. Upload: marksheets, income certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), bank passbook, Aadhaar.
5. Submit before deadline (usually July-October for most schemes).
6. Track status on the portal.

Tips for Success:
- Apply early; many schemes have limited seats.
- Maintain original documents and certified copies.
- Keep bank account in student's own name (required for DBT).
- Check state-specific portals in addition to national portal.
- Reapply annually for renewal-based scholarships.

[Source: National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in), AICTE Pragati Guidelines 2024, DST INSPIRE Scheme Notification, Maulana Azad Education Foundation, UGC Scholarship Guidelines]
