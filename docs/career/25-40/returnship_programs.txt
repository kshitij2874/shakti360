Returnship and Re-entry Programs for Women in India (Ages 25-40)

Career breaks are common for women due to maternity, caregiving, relocation, or family responsibilities. Returnship programs are structured re-entry pathways that help women restart professional careers after a gap, typically offering mentorship, training, and a path to full-time employment.

What is a Returnship?
A returnship is a professional internship for experienced individuals returning to work after a career break (usually 1-10 years). Unlike entry-level internships, returnships recognise prior experience and offer roles at mid-to-senior levels with competitive compensation.

Major Returnship Programs in India:

1. Tata Group — Second Careers, Inspiring Possibilities (SCIP):
- Across Tata companies (TCS, Tata Steel, Titan, Tata Motors, etc.).
- For women with 2+ years career break.
- Roles: engineering, IT, HR, finance, operations.
- Duration: project-based assignments leading to full-time offers.
- Apply: tata.com/careers.

2. TCS iON Rebegin:
- India's largest IT returnship program.
- For women with 1-8 years career break.
- Technical and non-technical roles across India.
- Training + mentoring + buddy system.

3. Infosys Restart With Infosys:
- For women in tech with 2+ years break.
- Roles: software development, testing, project management.
- Structured ramp-up period with dedicated mentors.

4. Wipro Begin Again:
- For experienced women professionals.
- Roles across technology and business functions.
- Mentorship and gradual ramp-up.

5. HCLTech — MPOWER (My Path of Work, Empowerment, and Re-entry):
- For women with STEM backgrounds.
- Training bootcamp + project assignment + full-time conversion.

6. Goldman Sachs Returnship:
- 12-week paid program in Bengaluru/Mumbai.
- For professionals with 2+ years break.
- Roles: technology, operations, compliance, finance.
- High conversion rate to full-time.

7. JP Morgan ReEntry Program:
- 15-week paid program.
- For experienced professionals with 2+ years break.
- Roles: technology, operations, risk, compliance.
- Mentorship + networking + skill-building workshops.

8. Unilever U-Restart:
- For women returning after maternity or caregiving breaks.
- Across marketing, supply chain, finance, HR.

9. Amazon Rekindle:
- For women with 1+ year career break.
- Roles: software development, operations, marketing.
- 16-week paid returnship with performance-based conversion.

Government and NGO Initiatives:

1. Ministry of Women and Child Development — Support Programs:
- Various skill training programs through National Commission for Women.
- Free digital literacy courses for women returnees.

2. NSDC (National Skill Development Corporation):
- Partners with companies for women's reskilling.
- Courses in data analytics, digital marketing, cybersecurity.
- Many courses free or subsidised.

3. JobsForHer (Herkey):
- India's largest women's career platform.
- Job listings specifically for women after career breaks.
- Mentorship networks and community events.
- Free resume reviews and career counselling.

4. SHEROES:
- Women-only platform with remote work opportunities.
- Community support for women professionals.

Freelancing and Remote Work Options:
- Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal: global freelance platforms.
- FlexiBees: India-focused, flexible work for women professionals.
- Consultancy projects through LinkedIn ProFinder.

Reskilling Resources:
- Google Career Certificates: data analytics, UX design, project management (6 months, Coursera).
- AWS re/Start: free cloud computing training.
- Microsoft LEAP: engineering apprenticeship program.
- NASSCOM FutureSkills: subsidised AI/ML/cloud courses.

Tips for a Successful Return:
1. Update LinkedIn profile highlighting transferable skills.
2. Address the career gap confidently — frame it as a deliberate choice.
3. Upskill in current industry trends before applying.
4. Start networking 3-6 months before target return date.
5. Consider part-time or freelance work to rebuild confidence.
6. Join women's professional communities (WomenWhoCode, Lean In circles).
